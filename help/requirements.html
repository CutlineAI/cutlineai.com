System requirements

What you need to run Cutline Beta: a Mac, Premiere Pro or After Effects 2022 or newer, and the Claude desktop app.

Quick startUser guideCommandsFAQTroubleshootingSystem requirementsLicence and devices

Computer

Requirement
Operating systemmacOS. Windows is coming soon.
ProcessorTested on Apple silicon. Intel Macs aren't confirmed yet.
Disk spaceA small install. Extra tools download when first needed, such as ffmpeg for silence detection (about 5 MB) and the tools for watching YouTube videos. Review previews use space in line with your media.
InternetNeeded for Claude and for Cutline's updates.

Adobe apps

AppVersion
Premiere Pro2022 (version 22.0) or newer, including Beta versions.
After Effects2022 (version 22.0) or newer, including Beta versions.
Colour add-onPremiere Pro 25.6 or newer. It uses Premiere's newer scripting.
Adobe Media EncoderNeeded for queued exports, sending After Effects renders to Media Encoder, and Review add-on previews. Not needed for direct exports.

You can use Premiere Pro, After Effects or both. Your Adobe apps need their own Adobe subscription.

Some features depend on what Adobe's scripting allows in your version. When something isn't possible in your version, Cutline tells you and explains how to do it by hand.

AI app

Cutline runs on your own AI account. AI usage isn't included with Cutline.

AI appStatusPlan
Claude desktop appSupportedA paid Claude plan is best: Pro, or Max for heavy editing.

ChatGPT support is coming soon.

Tips for your AI allowance:

  • Looking at frames, watching YouTube videos and reading very long timelines use more of it.
  • On long sequences, ask about a time range rather than the whole thing.

Licence

  • Terminal or command-line tools.
