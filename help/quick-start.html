Quick start

Cutline lets you edit by typing. You say what you want in the Claude desktop app. Cutline makes the edit in the Premiere Pro or After Effects project you have open, then tells you what changed.

What you need

  • A Mac. Windows is coming soon.
  • Premiere Pro or After Effects, 2022 or newer. You can use one or both.
  • The Claude desktop app, signed in to your Claude account. The AI runs on your own account, so a paid Claude plan is best.
  • The beta link you were sent.

You don't need a licence key during the beta.

Install

  1. Download the installer from the beta link you were sent.
  2. Double-click it. It adds Cutline to the Claude desktop app and installs the panel.
  3. Quit Premiere Pro or After Effects if it's open, then open it again.
  4. Choose Window > Extensions > Cutline Beta.
  5. Dock the panel somewhere out of the way and keep it open while you work.

When Cutline can work in that app, the top of the panel shows a green Ready.

Your first three requests

Open a sequence in Premiere Pro, then type these into Claude one at a time.

  1. What have I got selected? Cutline reads your timeline and tells you. It's a quick check that it's looking at the right sequence.
  2. Add a red marker at the playhead called "check audio" A small, safe change. The marker appears on your timeline.
  3. Cut out 1:20 to 1:45 and close the gap A real edit. Cutline reports the sequence length before and after, and anything that might now be out of sync.

The first time Cutline uses a tool, Claude may ask for your permission. Allow it.

Undo

Don't like a change? Say "undo that" in Claude, or click the undo button on the panel's Home tab. Either one takes back Cutline's whole last change.

Getting help

  • Panel not green? Start with Troubleshooting.
  • For more things to ask for, see Commands.
  • The User guide covers everything in detail.
  • Email hello@cutlineai.com with your Premiere Pro or After Effects version, what you typed and what happened.
