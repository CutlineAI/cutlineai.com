# System requirements

What you need to run Cutline: a Mac, Premiere Pro or After Effects, and the Claude desktop app on a paid Claude plan.


## Computer

| | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Operating system** | macOS. Windows is coming soon. |
| **Processor** | Tested on Apple silicon. Intel Macs aren't confirmed yet. |
| **Disk space** | A small install. Extra tools download when first needed, such as ffmpeg for silence detection (about 5 MB) and the tools for watching YouTube videos. Review previews use space in line with your media. |
| **Internet** | Needed for Claude and for Cutline's updates. |

## Adobe apps

| App | Version |
| --- | --- |
| **Premiere Pro** | The current version from Creative Cloud. Cutline is tested on the latest versions (October 2026); tell us if your version gives you trouble. |
| **After Effects** | The current version from Creative Cloud. Cutline is tested on the latest versions (October 2026); tell us if your version gives you trouble. |
| **Colour add-on** | Premiere Pro 25.6 or newer. It uses Premiere's newer scripting. |
| **Adobe Media Encoder** | Needed for queued exports, sending After Effects renders to Media Encoder, and Review add-on previews. Not needed for direct exports. |

You can use Premiere Pro, After Effects or both. Your Adobe apps need their own Adobe subscription.

Some features depend on what Adobe's scripting allows in your version. When something isn't possible in your version, Cutline tells you and explains how to do it by hand.

## AI app

Cutline runs on your own AI account. AI usage isn't included with Cutline.

| AI app | Status | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Claude desktop app** | Supported | A paid Claude plan is best: Pro, or Max for heavy editing. |

ChatGPT support is coming soon.

Tips for your AI allowance:

- Looking at frames, watching YouTube videos and reading very long timelines use more of it.
- On long sequences, ask about a time range rather than the whole thing.

## Licence

You need a Cutline licence key, or nothing at all for the 7-day free trial. See [Licence and devices](licence-and-devices.html).

## Not needed

- Terminal or command-line tools.
- Adobe debug mode or developer settings.
- A separate Adobe extension installer.
