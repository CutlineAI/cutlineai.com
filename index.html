The middle lane

Some editors won't let AI near a timeline. Others let one-click apps cut the whole thing, and you can tell. Same zooms, same captions, same edit as everyone else.

Cutline sits in between. It does the clicking. You make the calls. Every change lands as normal clips, keyframes and markers in your own project, so you can change any of it by hand, or take the whole thing back with one click.

How it works

  1. Type it

    In the Claude desktop app, in plain English. “Remove every gap in this sequence.” “Duck the music under the dialogue.”

  2. It edits

    Cutline makes the change in the sequence or comp you have open. Not a copy, not a web app. Your project.

  3. It tells you

    It reads the result back and lists exactly what moved. Not right? One click undoes Cutline’s whole last change.

What it can do

Premiere Pro

Cuts, trims, ripples, gaps and silences, markers, titles and effects, keyframes, audio ducking, project housekeeping and exports. More than 110 tools.

After Effects

Comps and layers, keyframes and easing, expressions, text, effects, masks and the render queue. More than 50 tools.

Cutline Context

Drop in a brief, a style guide or a script. Cutline reads it before it edits and tells you what it’s following.
