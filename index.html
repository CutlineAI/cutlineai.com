Everyone knows AI edited that.

They don’t have to.

Some editors won’t let AI near a timeline. Others hand the whole edit to a one-click app, and you can tell: same zooms, same captions, same cut as everyone else.

Cutline sits in between. It does the clicking. You make the calls. Every change lands as ordinary clips, keyframes and markers in your own project, so you can change any of it by hand, or take the whole thing back with one click.

It lives next to your timeline

Cutline Beta docks as a panel in Premiere Pro and After Effects. It shows what you have open, the last change it made and a button to take it back. You talk to it from the Claude desktop app, the same way you’d brief an assistant editor.

It never saves your project unless you ask, keeps a backup of the sequence before big batch edits, and asks before anything risky.
