Features

Everything Cutline does today, and what’s coming next. Every edit happens in your own project and comes back with a report of what changed.

Premiere Pro

Editing

The cutting itself, in your real timeline.

  • Razor at any time, on all tracks or chosen ones
  • Remove a range and close the gap, or leave the gap
  • Ripple delete or lift by selection, name or position
  • Trim heads and tails; roll, slip and slide
  • Move and copy clips; replace a clip in place
  • Turn clips on or off over a range to switch angles
  • Place items by overwrite or insert; link and unlink
  • Rename clips with a pattern; select by name, label, range or track
  • Freeze frames, match frame, nest and un-nest

Gaps and silences

Clean-up passes on long timelines.

  • Find gaps per track, or where every track is empty
  • Close gaps while keeping tracks in sync
  • Find silences across mic tracks, then remove, lift, disable, cut or mark them

Sequences and tracks

Building and managing sequences.

  • Create, duplicate, open, close and delete sequences
  • Make a new sequence from a time range of another
  • Sequence settings, start timecode, in and out points, work area
  • Add, delete, rename, lock, mute, hide and target tracks
  • Auto Reframe a copy to 9:16 or any ratio
  • Scene Edit Detection to markers or cuts
  • Timeline summary; move the playhead, find the next edit, play and stop

Markers

Sequence and clip markers.

  • Add, list, change and delete markers by colour, name, time or type
  • Chapter markers
  • Clip markers

Titles, effects and motion

Graphics, effects and keyframes.

  • Motion Graphics templates and their text
  • Adjustment layers
  • Apply, change and remove effects, including third-party ones
  • Copy effects and keyframes between clips
  • Crop, position, scale, rotation, opacity and blend mode
  • Keyframes on any parameter, with interpolation
  • Basic Lumetri by value; Warp Stabilizer
  • Speed, reverse, frame blending and optical flow

Audio

Levels and mixing basics.

  • Clip volume in dB, pan, volume keyframes and fades
  • Duck music under dialogue automatically
  • Audio effects and crossfades

Project and media

The housekeeping nobody wants to do by hand.

  • Import files, folders and image sequences into bins
  • Import XML, EDL, AAF or another Premiere project
  • Create, search, move, rename, label and delete bins and items
  • Reports on offline, unused and duplicate media
  • Consolidate duplicates; collect the project and media into a folder
  • Metadata, XMP and custom fields
  • Interpret footage; relink, refresh and proxies
  • Subclips, poster frames and caption tracks
  • Project settings; clear preview files to free space

Export and handoff

Getting the edit out.

  • Export with any preset, directly or through Media Encoder
  • Queue clips or files in Media Encoder and track progress
  • XML for Resolve, AAF or OMF for audio post
  • Save stills, and show any frame to the AI so it can see the shot

After Effects

Comps and layers

Building and organising comps.

  • Create, duplicate and change comps; precompose
  • Add solids, text, nulls, shapes, adjustment layers, cameras and lights
  • Duplicate, delete, reorder and rename layers; switches, labels and parents
  • Trim, move, stagger, stretch and time-remap layers

Animation

Keyframes and expressions.

  • Transform values and keyframes
  • Set any property by its path
  • Add, remove and ease keyframes, from Easy Ease to hold and bezier
  • Add and clear expressions, with errors reported back

Text, effects and masks

Look and type.

  • Change text and its style: font, size, colour, stroke, tracking
  • Search, apply, reorder, toggle and remove effects
  • Essential Properties on template precomps
  • Rectangle and ellipse masks; track mattes
  • Comp and layer markers

Project and render

Files, settings and output.

  • Import files, folders, sequences and layered files
  • Organise, relabel, replace and delete items
  • Project settings: bit depth, colour space, expressions
  • Render queue or Media Encoder; render any frame for the AI

Add-ons

Cutline Context

Briefs, handbooks, scripts and notes Cutline reads before it edits.

  • Use them for this project, every project, or just the next request
  • Cutline tells you which ones it's following
  • Say “remember that…” to save a note for next time

Research

YouTube references

Read and watch YouTube videos without leaving your edit.

  • Details, licence, chapters and timestamped transcript
  • Contact sheets, shot lists, cut times and pacing
  • Search, including Creative Commons only

Always on

Undo and safety

Built so you can trust it with a real project.

  • One click undoes Cutline’s whole last change, even dozens of cuts
  • A backup copy of the sequence before big batch edits
  • Never saves your project unless you ask
  • Asks before risky deletes, like bins with clips in them or media used in sequences
